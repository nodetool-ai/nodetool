NodeTool ships examples for every editor, not only workflows. Open the
**Examples** page from the **More** panel, or choose **Browse examples** on the
new-project screen. Each tab adds an editable copy to your project, so the
shipped original never changes. The
[Templates Gallery]({{ '/templates-gallery' | relative_url }}) covers the
page itself.

| Tab | What you get | Opens in |
|---|---|---|
| [Apps](#apps) | A screen with inputs and a Run button, plus the workflows behind it | [Mini Apps]({{ '/mini-apps' | relative_url }}) |
| [Workflows](#workflows) | A node graph you can run and edit | [Workflow editor]({{ '/workflow-editor' | relative_url }}) |
| [Storyboards](#storyboards) | A board of shots with briefs, ready to render | Storyboard |
| [Timelines](#timelines) | A finished film with every clip, text layer, and keyframe editable | [Video editor]({{ '/video-editor' | relative_url }}) |
| [Sketches](#sketches) | A 1200 × 900 image document with five vector layers | [Sketch editor]({{ '/sketch-editor' | relative_url }}) |
| [3D models](#3d-models) | Low-poly glTF models in six packs | [3D editor]({{ '/3d-editor' | relative_url }}) |
| [Games](#games) | A playable 2D or 3D game | [Game editor]({{ '/game-editor' | relative_url }}) |

> To make something new from a brief rather than open an example, see the
> [Cookbook]({{ '/cookbook' | relative_url }}). It names the guided flow or
> shipped skill for each kind of project.
{: .callout-tip}

## Apps

**Add to your apps** installs the app and the workflows it runs. The apps fall
into two groups.

**Studios** put a whole production chain behind one screen.

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| Film Studio | One brief drives a directed short, an editable rough cut, and its poster. |
| Trailer Room | Rewrite the beat sheet and shot list, then render and cut the trailer. |
| Concept Studio | Fan a brief into concept art, mix creatures, and develop the pick. |
| Directed Campaign Kit | One product image becomes a brief, three directions, and campaign formats. |
| Product Launch Kit | Stage a product photo into lifestyle mockups, then into video. |
| SKU Factory | One product photo becomes a cutout, studio shots, and a listing set. |
| Viral Ad Engine | One offer drives the ad copy, the hooks, and the video ad. |
| UGC Product Video | A native-audio testimonial with captions and a branded close. |
| Ad Maker | Compare copy routes and headline angles, then direct a campaign hero. |
| Product Price Drop | A layered six-second vertical ad from your product, logo, and prices, with no generated video. |
| Dubbing Desk | Transcribe a presenter clip, translate it, and voice it in a new language. |
| AI Video Post House | Restyle, grade, trim, and pull a cover still from one uploaded clip. |
| Podcast Production Desk | Clean a recording, then write show notes, a newsletter, and social posts. |
| Brand & Social | An agent reads your website and fills in your brand. |
| Photo Studio | A live photo editor and a batch retoucher. |
| Vary Image | Change one thing in an image, such as lighting or pose, and keep the rest. |
| Model Arena | Three models answer side by side, and a judge model scores them. |

**Ad formats** are sixteen apps that each build an editable 8 to 15 second
vertical ad from your images, logo, and copy. Examples include *Kinetic offer
wall*, *Product cutout shuffle*, *Editorial image panels*, and *Integration
puzzle that snaps together*. The [ad library](https://nodetool.ai/ad-library)
shows each format with a beat sheet and a sample cut.

## Recipes

A recipe is a named outcome built from one of the apps above and the ordered
workflows it runs. Each recipe page on the website walks through the steps and
shows a real run.

| Recipe | Outcome |
|---|---|
| [Directed campaign kit](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/directed-campaign-kit) | One product image to a campaign in four formats |
| [UGC product video](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/ugc-product-video) | A 15-second creator testimonial with captions |
| [Product photo to video ad](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/viral-video-ad-engine) | Compared copy and thumbnails, then an animated product shot |
| [Dub your video for a new market](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/multilingual-video-dubber) | A translated, re-voiced version of your clip |
| [One packshot, a full product set](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/ecommerce-sku-visual-factory) | Catalogue and campaign images from one photo |
| [Turn your story into a trailer](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/storyboard-to-trailer) | A storyboard, voiceover, and cut trailer |
| [Impossible product worlds](https://nodetool.ai/recipes/impossible-product-worlds) | A three-shot commercial around one impossible setting |

## Storyboards

Each board holds a brief, a style, and a shot list, ready to render stills and
then clips. Most are single-moment commercials and film scenes, such as *Cold
Brew Pour*, *Puddle Strike*, *Silent Arrival*, *Fox in Snow*, and *The
Interrogation*. Three are multi-shot openings to explore: *Sneaker Drop*,
*First Light*, and *Lighthouse Keeper*. The
[storyboard gallery](https://nodetool.ai/storyboards) shows each board with
its rendered shots. [AI Video Production]({{ '/ai-video-production' | relative_url }})
covers rendering and assembling a board.

## Timelines

Each timeline is a finished film in which every clip, text layer, and keyframe
stays editable.

| Film | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Serein | A 26-second software launch film from scene clips, text, and motion |
| Kite | A 15-second app ad made from motion graphics alone |
| Tidewater | A 16-second animated risograph poster |
| Cadence | A vertical year-in-review data story with animated charts |
| Prism | An 18-second running-shoe ad on a 120 BPM grid |
| Voltra | A 23-second motorcycle launch ad cut to the beat |
| T minus 30 | A 30-second launch broadcast with moving cameras and a live inset |

The agent can also insert stored compositions, such as a lower third, a logo
sting, a title card, or an end card, into any timeline. See
[Timeline Motion Design]({{ '/timeline-motion-design' | relative_url }}#composition-examples).

## Sketches

Sketches are layered image documents, filtered by category: NodeTool, Ads,
Movies, and Art studies. **Open sketch** creates your own copy. They need no
model or API key to open. See [Sketch Editor]({{ '/sketch-editor' | relative_url }}).

## 3D models

The **3D models** tab groups low-poly glTF models into six packs: Adventure,
Dungeon, Platformer, Seaside, Sci-fi, and Sci-fi outpost. **Open model** copies
the model into your project and opens it in the
[3D editor]({{ '/3d-editor' | relative_url }}).
