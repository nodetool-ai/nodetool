# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creators, directors, filmmakers, developers, and small production teams use
NodeTool to build repeatable multimodal work. They move between agent-guided
creation and direct editing, often in dense desktop workflows where project
context, model choice, cost, and intermediate results must remain visible.

## Product Purpose

NodeTool is an open-source, agent-first creative workspace for producing
images, video, audio, text, and applications. Agents and people operate the
same editable projects and workflows. Success means a creator can move from a
brief to a finished asset without losing context, surrendering provider choice,
or being locked into marked-up credits.

## Brand Personality

Capable, practical, and transparent. The product should feel like a focused
professional studio: dense when the work requires it, direct in its language,
and explicit about costs and tradeoffs.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS surfaces with oversized cards, decorative gradients, or loose
  spacing that reduces working density.
- Enterprise interfaces that bury common actions behind jargon or ceremony.
- AI hype aesthetics, including neon-on-black decoration, gratuitous motion,
  and unfamiliar controls where standard product patterns work better.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the work in focus. Navigation and chrome support the active creative
   task without competing with it.
2. Prefer familiar product patterns. Standard menus, lists, tabs, and controls
   should behave predictably.
3. Preserve context. Projects, documents, assets, and agent actions remain
   connected and easy to recover across sessions.
4. Make complexity inspectable. Show model, cost, state, and execution details
   when they affect a decision.
5. Use compact hierarchy. Dense professional workflows need clear grouping and
   restrained emphasis rather than large decorative surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA on every user-facing surface. Every interactive element has
a keyboard path and visible focus state. Maintain token-level contrast, do not
encode meaning through color alone, support text resizing, and respect reduced
motion preferences.
