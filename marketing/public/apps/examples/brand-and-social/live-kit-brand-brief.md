## **Voice & tone**
**Warm, optimistic, precise.** Aurora Labs speaks clearly and encouragingly, turning complex energy technology into friendly, human progress.

## **Color palette**
- **Aurora Green — #2E8B68 (primary):** Use for brand moments, buttons, headings, and signals of positive action.
- **Sunrise Gold — #F4B942 (complementary):** Add warmth to highlights, calls to action, and optimistic accents.
- **Sky Mist — #DCEFF0 (complementary):** Use for backgrounds, panels, and spacious, calming layouts.

## **Tagline options**
- Energy for a brighter home
- Smarter power, kinder living
- A brighter way to power home

## **Do / Don't**
- **Do:** Pair organic forms, soft light, and nature-inspired imagery with crisp diagrams and precise data.
- **Do:** Write in plain language, lead with human benefits, and make progress feel achievable.
- **Don't:** Use dark, dystopian climate imagery, harsh neon palettes, or overly technical visual clutter.
- **Don't:** Overpromise, guilt-trip people, or hide behind jargon when explaining the technology.
