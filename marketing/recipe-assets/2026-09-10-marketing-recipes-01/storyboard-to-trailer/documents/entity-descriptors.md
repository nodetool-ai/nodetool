# Entity descriptors and assignments

## TR-E1 Elin

Character. Original woman around forty, short dark hair with a few gray strands, lightly freckled face, calm direct expression, neutral soft daylight casting portrait. No resemblance to a real person. In every scene she wears a dark navy wool sweater under a faded mustard raincoat, with no hat, scarf, or visible logos.

Assigned to TR-S2, TR-S4, and TR-S6.

## TR-E2 North Point Lighthouse

Location. Modest cylindrical weathered white lighthouse on a low rugged headland, dark lantern roof, a small keeper's cottage beside it, blue-gray sea, and a narrow stone path. Building proportions stay consistent.

Assigned to TR-S1, TR-S2, and TR-S5.

## TR-E3 Keeper's Room

Location. Small stone-walled room with a weathered wooden table, a simple amber-shaded lamp on the left, one small window showing blue twilight, and restrained maritime details without text.

Assigned to TR-S3, TR-S4, and TR-S6.

## TR-E4 Message Bottle

Prop. Small clear weathered glass bottle, simple cork, folded cream paper inside, no label or embossed brand mark, photographed against a neutral background.

Assigned to TR-S2 and TR-S3.
