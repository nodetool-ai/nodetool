## LinkedIn post

A product image should carry more context than a blank text field.

Directed Campaign Kit uses that image as the starting brief. A multimodal language model identifies the product, drafts the audience and message, writes the headline and CTA, and proposes three creative directions. No image-generation call is spent until you approve one.

The chosen direction produces a hero, a 4:5 post, and a 9:16 story. An optional reference image can be limited to composition, palette, material, or lighting. One bounded revision stays beside the original instead of replacing it.

The result is a repeatable NodeTool mini app with selectable models, visible state, and workflows you can inspect and edit.

## 30-second demo script

Start on the empty app. Type an optional steer, then upload the product image.

The app reads the image and fills the brief: product, audience, campaign message, headline, CTA, and three creative directions.

Choose direction A, B, or C. Select the hero model and render.

Approve the hero to build a 4:5 post and 9:16 story. Then request one focused revision and compare it with the original.

One image started the campaign. The workflow keeps the direction, assets, and revision together.
